Matrix Analysis and Applied Linear Algebra by Carl D. Meyer (SIAM).

Course Description:   We study the theory of linear systems. Topics covered include general solutions of Ax=b, linear operators, vector spaces, norms, inner product spaces, orthogonality, determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and several matrix factorizations.
