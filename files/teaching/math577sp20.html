Course Description:   Numerical solution of initial-value problems for ordinary differential equations. Topics include concepts of stability, convergence, multi-stage methods, multi-step methods and stiff systems. Advanced topics like boundary value problems, differential-algebraic systems, delay differential equations, implicit-explicit methods etc. will be covered based on time and student interest.
